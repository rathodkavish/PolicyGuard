Policy Name: Enterprise Access Control & Identity Security Policy
Version: 3.2
Owner: Identity and Access Management Governance Team
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Review Date: 2026-12-31
Category: Access Control

1. Purpose and Scope
This policy defines mandatory identity governance and access security controls across all corporate systems, administrative consoles, and cloud infrastructure.

2. Policy Statements
- Privileged accounts must use multi-factor authentication.
- Administrative access must be reviewed every 90 days.
- Terminated users must have access disabled within 24 hours.
- Enabled accounts without activity for more than 45 days must be reviewed.

3. Enforcement and Compliance
Non-compliance with this policy will be flagged during continuous automated compliance checks and escalated to resource owners for remediation.
